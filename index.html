      How to play

      Each suspect matches exactly one thing from every column, and no two suspects share one.

      Read the clues and mark the grid: ✕ means "definitely not", ✓ means "this is it". When one square in a row of a box is ✓, the rest of that row and column must be ✕. When only one square is left without ✕, it must be ✓.

      The small boxes lower down link the other columns to each other, so you can chain facts: if Biscuit struck in the kitchen and Biscuit blamed the squirrel, then whoever struck in the kitchen blamed the squirrel.

      "Either A or B – but not both" means exactly one of the two is true. Fill in the case notes, answer the question, and press Check.

      Hint first points you to a useful clue; press it again to reveal a square.
